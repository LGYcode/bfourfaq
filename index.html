4. Ambient temperature sensor notes (please pay attention)

(1) The measuring range of the ambient temperature is 20–400°C. It will not display below 20°C.

(2) The ambient temperature sensor is embedded near the top of the probe's ceramic handle.

5. Common causes of abnormal temperature display

(1) It takes longer for heat to transfer through the ceramic handle to the sensor.

(2) Cold meat can conduct low temperature through the stainless steel probe to the sensor, affecting the initial reading.

This is a normal phenomenon. During low-temperature cooking, the ambient temperature reading will gradually become accurate as cooking time extends.

A: Our wireless probe is specifically designed for low-power long-term monitoring. Temperature readings are taken every 2 seconds and updated via Bluetooth. Its heating response speed cannot be directly compared to that of a plug-and-play instant-read thermometer, and the time required to reach thermal equilibrium is much slower. However, if you continuously monitor the temperature during cooking, its readings are accurate and reliable.

We recommend using the ice water calibration method for testing and verification:

1.Fill a glass with ice cubes.

2.Add cold water and stir for about 15 seconds.

3.Fully submerge the probe in the center of the ice water.

4.Let it sit for 15 seconds, then read the value.

Under normal conditions, the reading in the ice water mixture should be close to 0°C.

If the deviation is significant, please submit your issue through the Problem Feedback feature in the app, along with a screenshot of your order, and we will process it as soon as possible.
